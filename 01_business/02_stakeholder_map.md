# Stakeholder Map

> DRAFT – prepared with Claude, pending review by Kumar. Stakeholders and interviews are simulated.

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customers | Raise complaints | Fast, fair, clear outcome | Low (individually) | Voice via complaint data and survey themes |
| Front-line complaint handlers | Log, acknowledge, resolve | Workable process, clear authority to fix quickly | Medium | Interview, process walkthrough |
| Complaints team leader | Run daily operations | Backlog, deadlines, staffing | Medium | Weekly working session |
| Head of Customer Operations | **Sponsor** | Cost, speed, regulatory standing | High | Sign-off on BRD and business case |
| Compliance / Risk | Regulatory control | DISP deadlines, reporting accuracy | High | Review of process changes |
| Product owners | Own products generating complaints | Root causes, product fixes | Medium | Findings workshop |
| MI / Data team | Produce reporting | Data definitions, feed quality | Medium | Requirements for data and KPIs |
| IT | Systems and workflow tooling | Effort, change risk | Medium | Feasibility review |
| FCA | Regulator, publishes data | Fair outcomes, timely handling | High (external) | Inform only |
| Financial Ombudsman Service | Independent escalation route | Fewer referrals, fair outcomes | High (external) | Inform only |

## Notes
- Priority group: Head of Customer Operations, Compliance/Risk (high influence, direct impact).
- Interviews are simulated; add real or realistic quotes only if labelled as illustrative.
